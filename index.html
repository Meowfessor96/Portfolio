About me

I like figuring out how things work.

I’m a B.Tech Electrical Engineering student at IIT Hyderabad. I’m drawn to problems where I can follow an idea from a circuit or an algorithm to an experiment I can run and understand.

My projects span analog circuits, adaptive filtering, and software for students. I’m interested in what carries across those areas: breaking a problem down, testing each part, and making the result easy for someone else to use.

Outside coursework, I enjoy music, manga, and tinkering with apps to make them work the way I want.
